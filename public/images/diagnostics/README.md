## Image Guidelines
- Use high-resolution images (minimum 800x600 pixels)
- Ensure good lighting and clear focus
- Show multimeter readings clearly visible
- Include labels or arrows where helpful
- Use consistent styling across all images

## Video Guidelines
- Use clear audio and good video quality
- Show step-by-step procedures
- Include close-ups of important details
- Keep videos concise (2-5 minutes recommended)
- Add captions or voice-over explanations
